Respiratory Dynamics & Testing Guide
Tuned for 5 to 54 BrPM

• Rapid Breathing / Hyperventilation: Breathe rapidly (panting). Notice the green sine wave tighten into fast oscillations (up to ~54 BrPM / 1.1s cycle) without being drowned out by the 1.2 Hz heartbeat pulse.

• Slow Meditative / Pranayama Breathing: Take deep, slow breaths. Notice the wave smoothly stretch wide across the screen (down to ~5 BrPM / 12s cycle).

• Breath-Holding / Apnea: Hold your breath for >5.5 seconds. Notice the wave settle completely flat along the center dashed zero-baseline with status Holding breath / Steady.
